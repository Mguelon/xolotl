05Why it is new

Make it and cast it, on one GPU.

A character created and cast in a real video in one open app — no account, no credits, no upload.

The model’s pixels go to the person: tracked once, swapped in a crop at their own resolution, laid back over your frames at your clip’s size and aspect ratio. The sheet gives a performer who turns the character’s own back. Qwen3-VL writes what the character looks like into H3’s prompt.
